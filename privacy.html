Privacy Policy

Korea Patchwork

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Korea Patchwork does not collect any personal data. The app has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It makes no network requests of its own. Everything it shows you is stored inside the app on your phone.

What the app keeps on your phone

Four things are saved locally so the app remembers them between sessions:

  • Which language you chose.
  • Which places you added to “My trip”.
  • Which places you marked as visited.
  • Your trip plan: which day, and which part of the day, you put each place in.

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, it is gone.

Location

The “Nearby” screen can show which places in this guide are closest to you. To do that, the app asks your phone for your location at the moment you tap the button, and only while the app is open.

Your coordinates are used in your phone’s memory to calculate distances, and are then discarded. They are never written to storage and never transmitted anywhere — the app has no way to transmit them, because it does not connect to any server.

Location is entirely optional. If you decline the permission, every other part of the app keeps working normally.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies or similar technologies.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.
  • No downloading while you use it. Maps and photos are already inside the app.

Links that take you out of the app

Some buttons open something outside Korea Patchwork, and only when you tap them: map buttons (Naver Map, Google Maps, and on iPhone the “Open in maps” button, which opens Apple Maps), which pass a place’s name or location to that map service; phone numbers in Essentials, which open your phone’s dialler; source links on the credits screen, which open a website in your browser; and booking links, where a city page shows them, which open a travel partner’s website.

Once you tap one of those, you are in someone else’s app or website, and their privacy policy applies rather than ours. Some booking partners pay us a commission if you book through their link, and they may use a referral tag or cookie to attribute the booking. We never receive your personal details from them — only, at most, aggregate counts of bookings. Nothing about you is sent to them by this app.

Children

The app is a travel guide suitable for a general audience. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • Turn location off at any time in your phone’s settings for this app.
  • Erase everything you have saved with “Clear my trip” in the app’s Settings screen.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its stored data from your device.

Because the app holds no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request — the data lives only on your own device, under your control.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces anything that does collect data — advertising, for example — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here with a new date at the top.
